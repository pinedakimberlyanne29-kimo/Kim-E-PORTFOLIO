Field Study 1 & 2: Observing, Experimenting, Teaching

The e-portfolio of Kimberly Anne M. Pineda, education intern for Grade 7 General Science at Taguig National High School.

1Introduction

Welcome to my Field Study 1 and Field Study 2 e-portfolio. This portfolio records my observations, experiences, and learnings as a pre-service science teacher in a Grade 7 General Science classroom at Taguig National High School.

During Field Study 1, I focused on observing my cooperating teacher’s teaching strategies, classroom management, and interactions with learners. In Field Study 2, I became more involved in the teaching process by assisting with classroom activities, checking students’ outputs, recording their work, and preparing lesson plans and PowerPoint presentations with guidance.

This portfolio showcases my experiences, reflections, and progress as I continue developing my skills and preparing to become an effective and dedicated science teacher.

Brief biography

My name is Kimberly Anne Mediana Pineda, a fourth-year Bachelor of Secondary Education major in Science student at Taguig City University. I chose teaching because I want to share my knowledge, inspire students, and help them discover their potential. I chose Science as my major because I am curious about how the world works and I want to make learning science interesting and meaningful for my future students. One fun fact about me is that I enjoy creating learning materials and activities that make lessons more engaging and enjoyable.

Career objectives

To become a licensed science teacher who makes learning meaningful, engaging, and easy to understand through hands-on activities and real-life examples. To continuously improve my teaching skills through experience, reflection, and professional development while inspiring students to appreciate and explore science.

Personal educational philosophy

Every student has the ability to learn, grow, and discover new things when given proper guidance and opportunities. As a future science teacher, I aim to make lessons simple, engaging, and meaningful by connecting science concepts to students’ daily lives through hands-on activities, experiments, and classroom discussions. Creating a positive learning environment where students feel comfortable asking questions, sharing ideas, exploring their curiosity, and learning from their mistakes is important to me. Patience, understanding, and respect are values I want to practice in guiding learners with different needs and abilities. I also recognize the importance of continuous learning, accepting feedback, and improving teaching strategies to become a more effective educator. Through creative activities and appropriate learning materials, I hope to develop students’ critical thinking, problem-solving skills, and appreciation for science. Ultimately, my goal is to become a dedicated science teacher who inspires learners to enjoy discovering new things, believe in their abilities, and apply their knowledge to real-life situations.

4Cooperating School Profile

School profile

Taguig National High School (TNHS) is a public secondary school located along Imelda Romualdez Marcos Road, corner A. Reyes Street, Barangay New Lower Bicutan, Taguig City. It was established on January 8, 1980, and was formerly known as Fort Bonifacio College Annex before becoming Taguig Municipal High School. In 1994, it adopted its present name, Taguig National High School. The school provides secondary education and aims to support students’ academic development and personal growth.
